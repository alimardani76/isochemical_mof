\subsection{Post-freeze checks clarify descriptor attribution and uptake-basis robustness}

Additional submission-stage analyses were performed without changing the frozen
11,072-pair cohort, pair orientation, geometry threshold, confirmatory endpoint,
or primary estimand. Requiring the largest pairwise geometry difference to exceed
the second-largest by factors of at least 1.10, 1.25, and 1.50 retained 4,603,
2,866, and 1,715 pairs, with median primary responses of 0.414, 0.187, and
0.038 mmol g$^{-1}$, respectively; the corresponding positive-pair fractions were
66.3\%, 60.2\%, and 53.1\%. Thus, the stored pair ``driver'' should be read as the
descriptor with the largest observed geometry contrast, not as an isolated causal
perturbation. Strong responses commonly occur when several pore descriptors change
together.

The positive 65-bar CH$_4$ response also persisted across uptake bases. The frozen
gravimetric result was reproduced exactly (median 0.584 mmol g$^{-1}$; 70.5\%
positive). The volumetric response had a median change of 3.797 v/v units, 60.9\%
positive pairs, and a 95\% family-bootstrap interval of 3.438--4.185. The wt\%
representation was likewise positive (median 0.934 wt\%; 70.5\% positive). The
volumetric comparison is the more informative robustness check: density-related
normalization reduces pair-level sign consistency but does not reverse the
population-level result.

Same-source effects were strongly heterogeneous. DB0 showed a median primary
response of 0.342 mmol g$^{-1}$ (65.3\% positive), DB5 10.437 mmol g$^{-1}$
(99.4\% positive), DB6 -0.244 mmol g$^{-1}$ (34.2\% positive), and DB13
0.466 mmol g$^{-1}$ (70.2\% positive). The source populations also occupy very
different geometry regimes: DB5 has a median maximum pair difference of 0.958 and
72.2\% ASA/AVA-labelled pairs, whereas DB6 has a median maximum difference of
0.180 and only 8.0\% ASA/AVA-labelled pairs. Source identity is therefore
confounded with geometry magnitude and descriptor composition and should be treated
as an applicability boundary rather than a causal variable.

\subsection{Post-freeze submission robustness and structural case selection}

Geometry-attribution stability ranked the eight frozen pairwise descriptor
differences within each pair and compared the largest with the second largest.
Prespecified top-1/top-2 cuts of 1.10, 1.25, and 1.50 were applied without
reselecting pairs. Uptake-basis robustness re-linked the same frozen pairs to the
298 K, 65 bar methane records, required exact reproduction of the frozen mmol/g
pair effects, and then evaluated v/v and wt\% using the same orientation and
family-bootstrap procedure. Source-stratified analysis was restricted to same-source
frozen pairs and reported source populations with at least 100 pairs together with
their geometry-contrast and ASA/AVA composition.

Six structural examples were selected deterministically from the already-frozen
pair catalogue for visualization only: pressure-activated ASA, pressure-activated
AVA, positive-both accessible-space, negative-both diameter, pressure-suppressed,
and experimental-source strata. Selection targeted representative cases within each
predeclared stratum rather than the largest adsorption effect, no pair was used for
more than one panel, and the 12 corresponding CIFs were extracted with SHA256
checksums for rendering.
