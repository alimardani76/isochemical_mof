# Graphical abstract / TOC concept brief

## Recommendation

Prepare the concept now, but do not make final artwork a hard gate until the current Materials Horizons submission portal explicitly requests it or it is requested during revision/publication.

## One-screen concept

### Left — matched material family
Two simplified MOF structures:
- same recorded chemistry representation;
- same recorded topology/dimensionality;
- different pore geometry.

Label: **Chemistry + architecture matched**

### Middle — geometry-mode split

**Accessible-space ↑**
- surface / connected void
- favorable high-pressure CH4 regime

versus

**Characteristic diameter ↑**
- cavity / passage scale
- not universally favorable

### Right — operating context
Three compact controls:
- **loading / pressure**
- **density context**
- **process objective**

Show that the same structural direction can change value with state point and objective.

### Bottom concept line
**Pore geometry is a regime to target, not a scalar to maximize.**

## Do not make
- a miniature Figure 2/4 dashboard;
- six tiny Figure 5 crystal panels;
- a page of statistics;
- an audit-workflow graphic.

## Candidate TOC text
**Matched-family comparisons show that accessible space and pore diameter occupy different adsorption-response regimes, whose value is reorganized by loading, density, and process objective.**

## Shorter candidate
**MOF pore geometry behaves as a state- and objective-dependent regime rather than one scalar property to maximize.**
