# Materials Horizons positioning memo

## Editorial question

The likely initial-assessment question is:

> **Does this paper change how materials scientists should think about pore geometry, or is it mainly a rigorous database analysis?**

The manuscript should make the first interpretation easier than the second.

## Strongest conceptual pitch

The paper does not propose a better scalar descriptor or one universal pore optimum. It proposes a different design object:

> **a pore-geometry regime defined by geometry mode, geometry magnitude, structural/density coupling, operating state, and process objective.**

The matched-family design makes that conceptual statement credible. Global screening asks which descriptors predict performance across a heterogeneous library; this study asks what geometry-associated response recurs after major recorded chemistry and broad architecture are fixed before outcomes are inspected.

## Why the concept is broader than methane storage

The transferable insight is not the 65-bar CH4 number. It is that correlated pore descriptors should not be interpreted as one size axis; geometry mode and magnitude can carry different information; descriptor value can be state dependent; gravimetric and volumetric advantages are coupled through density; and the material objective can reorder the apparent benefit.

## Evidence that should lead the editor

1. Figure 2 — accessible-space vs diameter response separation.
2. S4/S5 robustness — class distinction survives cleaner attribution and uptake-basis changes while remaining density coupled.
3. Figures 3–4 — operating state and process objective reorganize the response.
4. Figure 5 — structural examples make the descriptor distinction chemically tangible.
5. Design rule — optimize a regime, not a scalar pore metric.

## What should not lead

- the 279,019-record database size;
- identifier harmonization mechanics;
- “11,072 pairs” as the novelty;
- long catalogues of secondary endpoints;
- GitHub/audit architecture;
- causal ASA/AVA claims;
- experimental-transfer claims.

## Desk-review risks

### Computational/database identity
Mitigation: New Concepts, cover letter, abstract/conclusion, and Figure 5 all lead with the materials-design concept.

### Limited experimental transferability
99.2% of pairs are hypothetical–hypothetical. Mitigation: state this openly and frame targeted experimental matched families as the decisive next test.

### Correlated descriptors
Largest-contrast labels are attribution classes, not isolated perturbations. Mitigation: H2 clean-dominance and Figure 5 already bound the interpretation.

### Communication length
PASS G1 removed roughly 15% of main prose while protecting the conceptual/chemical sections. Do not cut key chemistry merely to meet an arbitrary page target; use SI efficiently and test the final RSC template in PASS H.

## Submission narrative in one sentence

**Matched-family evidence shows that MOF pore geometry should be engineered as a state- and objective-specific multidimensional regime, not optimized as a universal scalar pore-size variable.**

## Recommendation

**Proceed with Materials Horizons as the first target.** The conceptual case is defensible; the remaining work is publication closure and forensic proofing, not analytical expansion.
